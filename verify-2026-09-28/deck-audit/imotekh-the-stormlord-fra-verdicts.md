## Verdicts

- Karn, Argent Defender — REJECT: "Artifacts and creatures entering the battlefield don't cause abilities to trigger" turns off the deck's own ETB engine (Noxious Gearhulk, Necron Deathmark, Solemn Simulacrum, Royal Warden's tokens, Triarch Praetorian's unearth draw) — same anti-synergy already flagged for Torpor Orb.
- Liliana the Repentant — ADD (swap #1).
- Hexhaven Dueling Arena — REJECT: "prepared" is a mechanic no card in this deck has; ETB-untapped colorless land with a dead ability.
- Mabel, Bitter Recluse — REJECT: "remove up to three counters from another target creature or planeswalker" is dead text — this deck runs almost no counters subtheme to interact with; as a vanilla 1-drop deathtouch body it doesn't clearly outclass Marionette Apprentice's fabricate+drain, which is already the weaker card being cut.
- Gallia, Tragic Host — REJECT (bench): "exile another creature card from your graveyard: return this card from your graveyard to the battlefield" is real (the exiled card, if an artifact creature, is itself an Imotekh trigger) but it competes directly with Theoretical Necromancer for the same graveyard-fodder role, and Theoretical Necromancer's ability returns a card to hand (recastable) rather than committing this creature back to the battlefield tapped; no clean out slot remains after swap #4.
- Multiply by Zero — REJECT: "base power and toughness 0/0 until end of turn" is a temporary Fog-style answer — the removal suite already runs 8 real destroy/exile effects.
- Terminal Criticism — REJECT: "Destroy target creature or planeswalker that's blue or red" is color-restricted; Bitter Triumph/Necron Deathmark/Dismember already answer any color unconditionally.
- Way of the Necromancer — REJECT: "empower Jace 2" and "put a loyalty counter on each planeswalker you control" is a superfriends payoff for a deck running zero other planeswalkers.
- Break Under Pressure — ADD (swap #5).
- Danitha, Spear of Agony — REJECT: "Whenever you cast a spell that targets an opponent or a creature an opponent controls, put a +1/+1 counter on Danitha" — this deck's removal mostly targets creatures generically, not always an opponent's, and there's no other payoff; a first-strike body alone doesn't beat anything kept.
- Loot, the Anomaly — REJECT: "Threshold — Sacrifice another creature or planeswalker: Loot gets -2/-0" shrinks its own power for a niche unblockable-math trick; too cute for the slot.
- Proft, Sinister Mastermind — REJECT: "You can't cast this spell unless there are seven or more cards in your graveyard" is a real cast restriction that strands it in hand early; too narrow.
- Tinybones, Pocket Nuisance — REJECT: "each opponent discards a card" plus damage-on-discard is a real engine but pure disruption with no artifact/graveyard synergy — same category this deck already declined (Solve for Disappointment, round 1).
- Way of the Deathbringer — REJECT: "empower Jace 5" and "Planeswalkers you control have [−2]..." is entirely off-plan superfriends value.
- Winter, Tormented Loner — ADD (swap #3).
- Traxos, Scourge Eternal — REJECT (bench): "Whenever you cast an artifact or creature spell, untap Traxos" is a real upgrade over the already-cut Traxos, Scourge of Kroog (only untapped on historic spells) — different enough from that HISTORY cut to reconsider, but it's a vanilla 4/4 trample beater with no recursion/drain text, and every OUT slot this pass found went to a card with a bigger engine attached.
- Massacre Girl, Most Wanted — ADD (swap #2).
- Archive Arbiter — REJECT: "Destroy target noncreature, nonland permanent. Or you gain 4 life" is generically fine but the 6-mana slot is already stacked (Marionette Master, Noxious Gearhulk, Their Name Is Death, Trazyn, The War in Heaven) and removal is already at 8 spells.
- Room of Refuge — REJECT: "prepared" mechanic again — dead ability, ETB-tapped land with no payoff.
- Afterthought Sentry — REJECT: "exile up to one target card from a graveyard" on attack is graveyard hate aimed at opponents with no benefit to this deck's own plan; weak body.
- Living Library — REJECT: "{6}, Sacrifice this creature: ...shuffles it into their library" is a slow, expensive answer with no other text.
- Medic's Kitesail — REJECT: "+1/+0 and has flying and... gain 1 life" on attack is a generic equipment with no evasive creature base to prioritize equipping.
- Rank Rat — REJECT: "each opponent discards a card" ETB only, no repeatable payoff, no synergy with the artifact-recursion plan.
- Silence the Echo — REJECT: "sacrifice a creature or planeswalker or pay {3}: Destroy target creature or planeswalker" is solid but the removal suite is already 8 spells deep and every open OUT slot in this pass went to a bigger engine piece instead.
- Cast Away Doubt — REJECT: "Draw two cards. ...deals 2 damage to each player" is strictly worse than the already-added Night's Whisper for this deck (extra mana, self-damage) with no upside kept.
- Screeching Soulbreaker — REJECT: "deals 1 damage to each opponent and you gain 1 life" on attack duplicates what Mirkwood Bats/Psychomancer already do in this deck; redundant.
- Theoretical Necromancer — ADD (swap #4).
- Extended Absence — REJECT (bench): "Exile target creature or planeswalker" dodges recursion better than the deck's -X/-X effects, but removal is already at 8 spells and no OUT slot remained after the top 5 picks.
- Rampart Hunter — REJECT: "target creature gets +2/+2 and gains deathtouch until end of turn" is a one-shot combat trick with no other text; doesn't beat anything kept.
- Apex Witchstalker — REJECT: gain-2-life-on-ETB/death and landcycling are fine flex, but it competes for an already-crowded 6-drop slot with nothing artifact/graveyard-specific to offer.
- Bloodline Recollector // Ancestral Craving [IF-OWNED] — REJECT (bench): "if three or more creatures died this turn... becomes prepared" is real in this sac-heavy shell, but it's rarity-uncertain and no OUT slot remains after the confirmed-owned picks above.
- Darklight Phoenix [IF-OWNED] — REJECT (bench): "if two or more creatures died this turn, return this card from your graveyard to the battlefield" is a strong repeating threat but not an artifact card itself (no direct Imotekh trigger), rarity-uncertain, and no slot remains.
- Overwrite the Multiverse [IF-OWNED] — REJECT: "Exile all creatures" removes this deck's OWN artifact creatures from the graveyard permanently, killing its own recursion targets — worse for this deck than Their Name Is Death/Phyrexian Scriptures, which only destroy.
- Emrakul, the Exigent Doom [IF-OWNED] — REJECT: MV10 colorless bomb competing with an already-full 6+ curve (Portal to Phyrexia MV9, Metalwork Colossus MV11); no artifact/graveyard synergy text.
- Hall of Echoes [IF-OWNED] — REJECT: "becomes a copy of target creature you control until end of turn" for {5} is a narrow, situational combat trick on a land; no payoff for this deck's plan.
- Dark Matter Manipulator [IF-OWNED] — REJECT (bench): "mill three cards" plus "+2/+0 for every seven cards in your graveyard" is a strong cheap enabler/threat, but rarity-uncertain and no OUT slot remains.
- Lich's Relic [IF-OWNED] — REJECT (bench): "for each opponent, destroy up to one target creature or planeswalker" is one-sided mass removal on reusable equipment — very strong if owned, but rare and no slot remains after the confirmed swaps.
- Extrapolate the Impossible [IF-OWNED] — REJECT: "reveal exactly two cards you own... from outside the game" needs an established wishboard this operator's paper collection has no evidence of; likely non-functional as written.
- Gideon the Oathless [IF-OWNED] — REJECT: pings only trigger off the OPPONENT's own creature ETBs/loyalty activations — reactive, no synergy with the artifact-recursion plan.
- Sanctum Lurker [IF-OWNED] — REJECT: "Planeswalkers you control have [+2]..." is off-plan superfriends value for a deck running zero planeswalkers besides an opponent-facing empower trigger it doesn't have.
- Rise of the Deathbringer [IF-OWNED] — REJECT: the -3/-3 mode kills this deck's own token army same as other rejected wipes; the draw mode is redundant with the draw suite already in place.
- Jhoira, Weatherlight Corsair [IF-OWNED] — REJECT: strong value but no artifact-recursion/graveyard text, and the 5-6 mana slot is already crowded; rare and no slot remains.
- Omnath, Locus of the Void [IF-OWNED] — REJECT: "gets +1/+1 for each unspent mana" wants a mana-sink plan this deck doesn't run — its mana is spent on removal/tutors/reanimation, not held.
- Avacyn, Angel of Horror [IF-OWNED] — REJECT (bench): "Whenever Avacyn or another nontoken creature you control dies, return that card to the battlefield... at the beginning of the next end step" is an excellent repeatable engine for this sac deck, but MV8/BBB, rare, and no OUT slot remains without cutting something load-bearing.
- Darksteel Angel [IF-OWNED] — REJECT: "can't lose the game and your opponents can't win the game" is a narrow anti-combo/anti-mill safety valve, not a proactive plan piece; doesn't advance the artifact-recursion game plan.
- Memnarch, the Warden [IF-OWNED] — REJECT: MV10 is a third ultra-expensive top-end card after Portal to Phyrexia and Metalwork Colossus; "draw a card for each artifact you control" is a huge payoff on paper but the curve doesn't have room for a fourth 9+ mana bomb, and rarity is uncertain.

46/46 cards verdicted.

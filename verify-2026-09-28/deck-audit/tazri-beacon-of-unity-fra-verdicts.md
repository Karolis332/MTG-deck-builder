# Tazri, Beacon of Unity -- Reality Fracture (unregistered/assumed) pass -- FULL 211-card colour identity

Baseline: deck after REFUTE.md Section 3's 6 KEEP swaps (Generous Gift, Putrefy, Infernal Grasp, Grand
Abolisher, Syphon Mind, Growth Spiral in; Concerted Defense, Bar the Gate, Multiclass Baldric, Stick
Together, Thwart the Grave, Explore the Underdark out; Selvala stays out, Feywild Caretaker stays in).
Section 6's Springleaf Parade/Realmwalker/Chomping Changeling recommendations were NOT applied to the
proposed list, so Solemn Doomguide, Archpriest of Iona and You Find a Cursed Idol are still the deck's
weak slots available for this comparison. Correction note: the first pass of this file covered only 13
colourless cards from a mis-scoped colour identity; this version covers the full 211-card WUBRG-legal set.

## Swaps

| # | IN | tier | OUT | why IN | why OUT is weaker |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Traxos, Scourge Eternal | ASSUMED | Solemn Doomguide | "Trample \| Traxos doesn't untap during your untap step. \| Whenever you cast an artifact or creature spell, untap Traxos." Colorless {4} fits any hand and the deck casts creatures/rocks every turn, so it keeps attacking | Solemn Doomguide is BB-intensive at 5cmc ("unearth {1}{B}" for graveyard party creatures) and does nothing until party creatures are already dead in the yard -- a narrow payoff the deck has no dedicated way to fill |
| 2 | Samut, Hazoret's Champion | ASSUMED | Feywild Caretaker | 2-mana Warrior/Cleric: "Creatures you control have haste" is a board-wide haste anthem that turns on every party creature and every fresh token (Karlach's second combat, Najeela's Warrior tokens, Outlaws' Merriment tokens) the turn it lands | Feywild Caretaker is a 5-mana 1/1 that only makes a 1/1 flier per turn while holding the initiative -- a much lower rate for more mana, and REFUTE.md already flagged it as the correct KEEP over Selvala, not a strong card in absolute terms |
| 3 | Rescue Girl, First Responder | ASSUMED | Archpriest of Iona | Flying Cleric, "{T}: Return another target permanent you control to its owner's hand" replays the deck's ETB/initiative creatures (Nadaar, Ravenloft Adventurer, Seasoned Dungeoneer, White Plume Adventurer, Acererak) for repeat value, and is itself a bigger, evasive body | Archpriest of Iona is a 1/1 whose only upside is "+1/+1 and flying" with a full party already assembled -- narrow and low-impact |
| 4 | Enlightened Confidant | IF-OWNED (mythic) | You Find a Cursed Idol | "Lifelink \| ...surveil 1. If you put a card with mana value less than or equal to the amount of life you gained... into your hand" is low-risk card advantage (lifelink caps the downside) the deck's draw package still lacks | You Find a Cursed Idol is a 3-mode 2cmc sorcery (destroy artifact / destroy enchantment / treasure+venture) where each mode alone is weak; the artifact/enchantment removal is already covered by Generous Gift/Putrefy |

Two near-misses noted but not swapped (see full reasoning in Verdicts): Samut, Tyrant of Naktamun (split
second is too narrow without a counter-war plan) and Seasoned Cryomancer (strong looting but UU-heavy,
IF-OWNED).

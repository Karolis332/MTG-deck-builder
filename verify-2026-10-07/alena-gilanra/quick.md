# Gilanra + Alena: quick swaps (2026-10-07, before the converged pass)

The deck has 5 pieces of cheap ramp (Sol Ring, Signet, Talisman, Llanowar Elves, Ruby) for an average MV of 5.66. The obvious fixes are cheap ramp in, plus the clearly worst cards out.

| # | Out | In | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bane of Progress | Nature's Lore | Bane says "destroy all artifacts and enchantments": that kills our own Sol Ring, Signet, Talisman, Monstrous Vortex, Warstorm Surge, Planetarium, both Gearhulks and Cyclops. Nature's Lore puts a Forest onto the battlefield untapped for {1}{G} on turn 2. |
| 2 | Rorix Bladewing | Selvala, Heart of the Wilds | Rorix is a vanilla 6/5 flying haste creature. Selvala: "{G}, {T}: Add X mana in any combination of colors, where X is the greatest power among creatures you control." In this deck that is 5–8 mana of any colour, and she draws when our fatty is the biggest creature. |
| 3 | Fling | Beast Within | Fling costs us our best creature. Beast Within is our only instant that answers any permanent: "Destroy target permanent." The deck has no other answer to enchantments. |
| 4 | Ulvenwald Observer | Farseek | Observer only draws when our creatures die. Farseek gets a Mountain on turn 2: red is the short colour (Burning Sun's Avatar, Drakuseth, Tyrant and Myojin cost RRR). |
| 5 | Diamond City | Sheltered Thicket | Diamond City makes only colourless mana in a deck with RRR and GG costs. Thicket is "{T}: Add {R} or {G}" and has cycling {2} once lands are no longer needed. |

The full pass (land count, stop proofs, gate) follows in `build-report.md` / `proposed.txt`.

> **Superseded** by `build-report.md` §2 / `proposed.txt`: the converged pass keeps Selvala, Beast Within and Farseek, but sends Bane out for Cleanup Crew, Sakiko for Selvala, Rorix for Nature's Lore, Ruric Thar for Farseek, Ulvenwald Observer for Vastlands Scavenger, and Diamond City for Gruul Turf.

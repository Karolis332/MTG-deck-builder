How it works

  1. 1

    Pick a commander

    Choose a commander and a format: Commander (EDH), Brawl (Arena Historic Brawl), Standard Brawl.

  2. 2

    Set your pool and power level

    Use your collection or the full card pool. Commander bracket checks follow Wizards' official Commander Bracket rules.

  3. 3

    Get a deck with reasons

    Get a complete deck in 30–90 seconds, with a stated reason for every card.

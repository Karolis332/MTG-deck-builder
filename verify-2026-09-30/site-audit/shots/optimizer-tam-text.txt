copy limit are always cut. Then role quotas: when a deck runs more removal, draw or ramp than the format's band, the weakest cards in that role are flagged. In commander formats, cards with no synergy edges to the commander or the rest of the deck rank next; in 60-card formats, cards absent from the current meta top list. Lands are never cut here: the land target uses Karsten's formula (lands = 19.59 + 1.90 × average mana value − 0.28 × cheap ramp/draw spells (60-card deck)).\n\nAdds come from role gaps, cards that co-occur with yours in nearly 4 million public decks, commander inclusion rates, and the format's meta staples.

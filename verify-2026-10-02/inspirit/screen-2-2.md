## CANDIDATEs, ranked

1. **Jazal Goldmane** ({2}{W}{W}, First strike; "{3}{W}{W}: Attacking creatures you control get +X/+X until end of turn, where X is the number of attacking creatures") — amplifies the locked Depthshaker Titan alpha turn directly: with 7+ attackers (5 spares, Traxos, Kilo, Depthshaker, Urza's Construct per the build report's math) it adds +7/+7 or more to every attacker on top of the existing ~73 trample total, for 5 extra mana during combat. Vulnerable as a nonartifact creature before the kill turn and needs WW (the deck's lightest colour), which is the case against it.

## 3. Stop proof (pass 2, after both swaps: no new card beats the weakest kept card of its role)

**Meren** (BG). In-identity candidates: Liliana, Bounty Hunters (placed), Invigorator, Fulcrum, Puppetbeast, Fen,
Cemetery.
- Engine role, weakest kept after the swap:
  - The Gitrog (operator keep).
  - Long Feng ("put a +1/+1 counter on target creature": the only Broodscale-loop partner besides Mazirek; Bounty
    Hunters cannot put counters on Broodscale).
  - Golgari Charm (Culling Ritual regenerate lock).
  - None is beatable by the rest.
- Liliana vs graveyard-role peers (Buried Alive, Grave Researcher, Emeritus): her mill is mandatory and each Spawn
  enters, so she caps the loop. She went to Imotekh (bigger gain).
- Wipes: Fulcrum is weaker than all three kept ones. Toxic Deluge is one-mode cheap. Massacre Wurm hits only
  "creatures your opponents control". Culling Ritual is a lock.
- Invigorator: no damage source.
- Puppetbeast: Meren's artifacts are Sol Ring, Signet, Talisman, Cauldron and Greaves, so there is nothing to sacrifice.
- Fen and Cemetery: singleton.

**Imotekh** (B). Candidates: Liliana (placed), Fulcrum. Weakest kept after the swap: Wayfarer's Bauble, Wishclaw
Talisman, Myr Retriever.
- Fulcrum vs Bauble. Bauble is ramp ("put that card onto the battlefield tapped"); Fulcrum's loot is not.
- Its +2 Colossus discount equals Thought Vessel's, which is already in the deck.
- Its wipe is strictly worse than Their Name Is Death under Bio.
- vs Wishclaw: Wishclaw finds Colossus/Bio/Altar; Fulcrum does not.
- Result: no swap.

**Cabbage** (G). Candidates: Invigorator, Fulcrum, Puppetbeast (kept). Weakest kept: Brawn, Autumn's Veil, Ghalta the
Unstoppable (09-28 weakest #2–4).
- Invigorator vs Ghalta: a 6/6 vigilance with no Food/token synergy. Ghalta gives "Other creatures you control have
  trample" to the Beast swarm.
- Invigorator vs Wild Growth/Cultivate (ramp): its ramp needs damage dealt to it; Cultivate is unconditional.
- Fulcrum vs Veil/Brawn: a 7-mana total reset that kills the engine. The deck has 0 wipes, but this one does not fit.
- Puppetbeast vs ESG: kept, per 09-28 Fable §3.

**Ramos**: no kept cards, so nothing to beat. 15 cards fit its identity only, plus Bounty Hunters and Invigorator, which
also fit Meren/Cabbage. None is a Gate, so nothing joins the reservation stub. The 5c duals (Fen, Cemetery, Shipwreck
Marsh, Annex) and the gold cards are candidates for the real Ramos build.

**Risks to note on the two swaps**
- Liliana's mill is mandatory. Sacrifice her (Altar/Zealot) when the library is ≤ ~25.
- With 09-28 row 3 Smothering Abomination ("Whenever you sacrifice a creature, draw a card"), each Colossus-loop
  iteration draws 3 and mills 6. Stop the loop with ≥ 9 cards left.
- Bounty Hunters: the earthbent land returns tapped, so point earthbend X at an already-tapped land. Under Mikaeus
  ("Other non-Human creatures… +1/+1") the X=0 land lives as a 1/1 haste body, which is sac fodder.

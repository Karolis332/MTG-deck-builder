## The Black Grimoire v1.0.0-beta.3

- **Sign in with your username OR your email.** The desktop account is local to this PC —
  created in the setup wizard — and is separate from any theblackgrimoire.com login.
- **Deck Score v1.2 (beta)** now shows in the Score tile for Commander and Brawl decks
  (marked provisional — the scoring model is still calibrating). Standard decks keep the
  existing score for now.
- **Match sync to the web** (from beta.2): Settings → Web Sync sends your Arena match
  history to your theblackgrimoire.com dashboard once you paste a sync token there.

### Installing (unsigned beta)

This release isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen will warn on first run.
Click **More info → Run anyway** to proceed.

List: verify-2026-10-08/best-deck/beledros-recommended.txt (100, draft until sleeved). 35 lands + Revitalizing Repast MDFC. Built by the collection-wide search (scripts/collection-best-deck.ts, objective 100.8) and hand-adjusted so Tam, Tazri and Bumi give nothing. Oracle quotes are DB text.

1. Plan in 3 lines

  • Beledros Witherbloom is a fodder faucet: "At the beginning of each upkeep, create a 1/1 black and green Pest creature token with 'When this token dies, you gain 1 life.'" In a 4-pod that is four Pests per round without spending a card.
  • Every Pest death is a drain (Zulaport Cutthroat, Blood Artist, Cauldron of Essence, Mirkwood Bats) and a life-gain event (Dina, Soul Steeper, Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose, Sanguine Bond turn each gain into more loss).
  • Close by attrition, by one Gray Merchant of Asphodel burst through Vito or Sanguine Bond, or by a pumped alpha strike (Thunderfoot Baloth, Blossoming Bogbeast). No infinite combo: the deck wins by never running out of deaths.

Does NOT work the way it looks

  • ❌ Deadly Tempest hits you too: "Each player loses life equal to the number of creatures they controlled that were destroyed this way." Pests refund 1 each, nontoken creatures do not. Count before casting.
  • ❌ Lightning Greaves shroud stops Witch's Clinic ("{2}, {T}: Target commander you control gains lifelink") from targeting Beledros. Guardian Augmenter hexproof does not.
  • ❌ Toxic Deluge life is paid, not gained: no Vito, Dina or Sanguine Bond triggers.
  • ⚠️ Blood Artist says "target player loses 1 life": one opponent, not each. Zulaport and Cauldron are the table drains.
  • ⚠️ Morbid Opportunist "triggers only once each turn"; Midnight Reaper is "nontoken". Token deaths draw through Moldervine, Liliana and Plumb only.
  • ⚠️ Lifegain hate (Erebos, Tainted Remedy) switches off lines 1 and 2 through Dina, Vito and Bond. Zulaport, Cauldron, Bats and Merchant's own loss still work.

6. Lines to visualise each turn

  • Upkeep (every player's): a Pest arrives. Bats drain on creation. Decide at once whether it dies now (Seer/Altar/High Market) or waits for Priest or Braids.
  • Your end step: Witch of the Moors needs a life-gain event this turn; Pestfinder prepares; Braids sacrifices the Pest.
  • Hold up: Veil of Summer on the Merchant or Mikaeus turn; Imp's Mischief against the removal aimed at Vito or Mikaeus; Tyvar's Stand and Warg Tactics mode 2 for a single piece; Infernal Grasp and Assassin's Trophy at instant speed.
  • Wipes: sacrifice the board in response for drains and Mikaeus returns. Massacre Wurm is one-sided: your Pests survive. Deluge kills your Pests too, so cast it with Zulaport or Cauldron out.
  • Beledros untap: only when the lands untapped are spent the same turn and the life returns the same turn (Merchant, Priest, Pest sacrifices into Dina/Vito).

7. Misplays to avoid

1. Casting Deadly Tempest with eight nontoken creatures and 12 life.

2. Equipping Greaves to Beledros and then trying to give him lifelink with Witch's Clinic.

3. Expecting Humans (Zulaport, Priest, Witness, Opportunist, Ophiomancer, Witch) to return from Mikaeus undying.

4. Paying 10 life for the untap with nothing to cast or no drain to refund it.

5. Tapping Idol only in your own turn. Any upkeep Pest unlocks it.

6. Letting Alhammarret's Archive double a long Plumb or Moldervine chain into an empty library.

7. Bojuka Bog "exile target player's graveyard" on yourself with Merchant and Witness in the yard.

8. Killing your own Pests into Blood Artist alone (one opponent) when Zulaport or Cauldron (each opponent) is a turn away.

Pilot rules

  1. Before combat, never tap a land creature for mana unless Bumi will connect: a tapped land creature cannot attack, and Bumi's trigger untaps it for the land-only combat.
  2. Earthbend Rumble Arena first: it has vigilance, so as a land creature it attacks and still taps for mana (CR 702.20b).
  3. Temur Battle Rage goes on Bumi, not on a land: each Bumi damage step adds one more land-only combat (CR 500.8).
  4. Cast Awakening of Vitu-Ghazi after Bumi's untap and before the extra combat: the 9/9 has haste and joins the land-only attack.
  5. Earthbend before Overrun and Germination Practicum: their pumps only apply to creatures on the battlefield at resolution (CR 611.2c).
  6. Halana and Alena, Partners trigger at the beginning of every combat, Bumi's extra combat too. Target a land creature, because Bumi cannot attack in that combat.
  7. Hold Silkguard or Snakeskin Veil for removal aimed at Bumi on the kill turn. If an earthbended land dies anyway, it returns tapped.
  8. Sacrifice an earthbended land to Fling or Roiling Regrowth: it comes back tapped. You lose the counters, not the land.

Mulligan

✅ Keep

  • 3–5 lands and any 6-drop: keep (the sim's keep rule).
  • 2 lands plus Llanowar Elves, Poison Dart Frog, Ilysian Caryatid, Tangled Florahedron, Sol Ring or Three Visits: keep.
  • 3 lands plus Gilanra, Caller of Wirewood's third land and a landcycler such as Slavering Branchsnapper: keep, because the cycler is the 4th land.
  • 4 lands plus Soul of the Harvest or Primordial Sage: keep; the draw engine lands on T4–5.

❌ Mulligan

  • 0–1 land, even with a mana creature.
  • 2 lands and no ramp piece of mana value 1–2: a landcycler costs the whole T2 and still leaves the hand short.
  • 6–7 lands: the first mulligan is free in a pod.

Urza, Lord High Artificer

mono-U artifactscommanderproposed

Every artifact is a mana source and Construct power under Urza, Lord High Artificer. Cast rocks and tokens to grow N (artifacts controlled), copy the Construct with Worldwalker Helm or Ember Island Production, then close with Sun Quan, Lord of Wu's horsemanship, Super State, or a kicked Rite of Replication on Whirler Rogue. The Legend of Kuruk buys a second alpha. The box was a Talrand spells shell (13 instants/sorceries, 24 artifacts); it loses to the artifact half, 26 artifact spells to 20 instants/sorceries in the final list, so the spell package was cut wholesale and the deck rebuilt around Urza's mana engine.

Cards that stay (and why)

Spelunking

Its ruling: lands simply put onto the battlefield tapped (Farseek, Kodama's Reach, the Courtyard/Hideout fetches) enter untapped, and self-tapping lands (tri-lands, a shock you don't pay 2 life for) can enter untapped because you choose the order of the two effects.

Stick Together

It is symmetric (each player keeps up to one Cleric, Rogue, Warrior and Wizard), so it hurts the go-wide board too. It earns its slot when the board is already against you: you keep a full party of four, while opponents whose creatures are not party types keep fewer. Never cast it while ahead on board (pilot guide).

Coveted Prize

It tutors any card to hand; with a full party it then casts a spell of mana value 4 or less from hand for free (the tutored card may be that spell). It most often finds Blade Historian, Grand Abolisher or Destined Warrior, not the whole combo, since the ruling confirms the free cast happens on resolution and X must be 0 (no free Springleaf Parade).

Linvala, Shield of Sea Gate

Looks like a plain 3-mana flyer, but her full-party ability can be pointed at a mana rock or the best blocker on the alpha turn, and she sacrifices herself for hexproof or indestructible when the board is under pressure.

Mirror Entity

A mana sink that only shines with the Springleaf Parade token army; at X=0 it kills your own board, since it overwrites base power and toughness to 0/0 (Scryfall ruling). Activate after blocks, with X high enough that Tazri (4/6) and Zagras (4/4) do not shrink.

Pilot rules

  1. Activate Tazri's dig at the end of an opponent's turn, before yours; it has no timing restriction.
  2. Fill missing party slots with the dig before digging for Najeela, Karlach or Blade Historian.
  3. Commit 4-5 bodies to the board, but keep 1-2 party creatures in hand against a sweeper; Tazri's dig rebuilds the rest.
  4. Attack order: combat triggers resolve, then everything except Najeela/Karlach/Ingris attacks, then Najeela's tokens, then blocks, then Mirror Entity's X, then damage, then Najeela's end-of-combat activation.
  5. Never tap a Parade or Najeela token made this turn for mana without haste (CR 302.6).
  6. Hold Heroic Intervention or Selfless Spirit until the board is fully committed, then fire it against a destroy-based wipe.

The engine

Every loyalty ability gains you 1 life (Paradox), and every life gain puts +1 loyalty on every walker (Mentor). So each activation also pumps the whole team. These cards gain you life too, and each gain triggers Mentor again:

Way of the Paradox
Every loyalty ability you use gains you 1 life.
Way of the Mentor
Every time you gain life, put +1 loyalty on every walker.
Liliana the Faultless
1 life whenever a walker or creature enters.
Interplanar Beacon
1 life per walker you cast.
Sorin, Vengeful Bloodlord
Your walkers have lifelink on your turn.
Kaya, Intangible Slayer
Her +2 drains each opponent for 3 and gains you life.
Ob Nixilis of the Black Oath
His +2 drains each opponent for 1 and gains you that much.

How you win

1. Jace wipes out their libraries
Jace, Reality Sculptor's 0 ability works once your Jaces have 25+ loyalty combined. It exiles all but the bottom card of each opponent's library, so they lose on their second draw. The Ways add 2–7 to the Jace token each, Protege's Awakening and Vraska's Final Mercy add 6, Avatar adds 2 per land, and Tam plus Mentor keep pumping it. Jace, Memory Adept is a second Jace, so its loyalty counts too, and its 0 mills 10 to speed the finish. Entrust the Spark puts Jace straight onto the battlefield, and Pia finds a Way.
2. Poison kill
Vraska, Betrayal's Sting −9 puts one player at 9 poison. Any proliferate finishes them: Vraska's own 0, Tam's ability, an Evolution Sage landfall or a Thrummingbird hit.
3. Dragon attack
Sarkhan the Masterless +1 turns every walker into a 4/4 flying Dragon. Then Ajani Unrelenting +1 gives them haste and +1/+0, and each activation also makes a Cadet. Five walkers means 25 flying damage.
4. Ultimates
With the engine running, every walker climbs 2–3 loyalty a turn. Main targets: Elspeth −7 (+2/+2 and flying for your tokens), Chandra −7, Ajani Resolute −10, and Kaya +2 draining each opponent for 3 every turn.
5. Drain
Quintorius drains 2 per opponent whenever you cast from exile, which Chandra's +1 does every turn. Ob Nixilis, the Hate-Twisted deals 1 to an opponent for every card they draw, so Jace, Memory Adept's −7 (each opponent draws 20) deals 20 to each of them.

Removal 9 · Toxic Deluge, Settle the Wreckage, Fumigate and Final Act kill creatures and leave your walkers standing. Fumigate's life gain also triggers Way of the Mentor.

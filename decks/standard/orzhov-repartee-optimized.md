## Game Plan

Repartee — Whenever you cast an instant or sorcery spell that targets a creature, put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control (Stirring Hopesinger). Attack early with 1-2 drop creatures (Scolding Administrator, Snooping Page, Conciliator's Duelist, Rehearsed Debater). Trigger repartee with targeted removal (Erode, Dissection Practice, Bitter Triumph, Killian's Confidence). Pay off repartee with counter-synergy cards (Lecturing Scornmage copies triggers; The Soul Stone creates tokens). Win through evasion, +1/+1 counter stacking, and token generation.

TMNT (Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles) crossover cards are ACTIVE in Arena Brawl despite Scryfall's legalities JSON showing `brawl: "not_legal"`.

**Why:** Arena has its own legality system that can differ from Scryfall, especially for crossover/Universes Beyond sets and event-specific cards. Scryfall's data reflects paper Magic rules, not Arena's digital-specific allowances.

**How to apply:** When building Arena Brawl decks, do NOT rely solely on Scryfall's `legalities.brawl` field to filter cards. TMNT cards (and potentially other Universes Beyond/crossover cards) may be playable. The user confirmed this by seeing opponents playing Michelangelo, the Heart in Arena Brawl matches.

**Cards confirmed playable:**
- Michelangelo, the Heart (added to Cabbage Merchant deck)
- Leonardo, the Balance (seen as opponent's commander)
- Super Shredder (seen as opponent's commander)
- Heroes in a Half Shell (seen as opponent's commander)

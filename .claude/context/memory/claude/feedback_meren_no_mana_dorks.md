In Meren of Clan Nel Toth decks, do NOT recommend vanilla 1-mana mana dorks (Elvish Mystic, Llanowar Elves, Birds of Paradise without abilities-on-death, etc.).

**Why:**
1. Meren IS the ramp — experience counters return creatures from grave for free, so she scales without dorks.
2. Vanilla 1/1 dorks become dead topdecks once Meren is online; they sac for a counter but give no value on death.
3. BG sees heavy removal/sweepers at bracket 2-3 — dorks evaporate, leaving you ramp-less.
4. Better fodder = creatures with ETB/death triggers (Boggart Trawler draws, Saw in Half doubles deaths) or sac-outlets (Ashnod's Altar) that double as combo enablers and survive wipes.
5. Black-green has stronger ramp options: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Cultivate-tier land ramp, Ashnod's Altar.

**How to apply:**
- When suggesting cards for any Meren-style grave/sacrifice deck, skip 1-mana vanilla dorks even if synergy scores rank them high.
- Prefer: ETB/LTB value creatures, rocks, sac-outlets, recursion enablers.
- This is Meren-specific — mana dorks remain fine in dedicated elfball or fast-combo lists.
- Confirmed during 2026-05-21 swap discussion: cut Elvish Mystic + Llanowar Elves for Ashnod's Altar + Victimize.
